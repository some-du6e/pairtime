# Erratum: SM 6.5's 20-second lookup at 20:02 is one 15-second slot

Date 2026-11-19. Raised by WP-FX2. Status: waiting for Karim. Until Karim decides, `testdata/timelines/6.5` keeps SM's row.

## Input

SM 6.5, row 20:02: "Reads, checks the phone view in the browser (20:02:30-:50, a bracketed lookup), types "still overlapping on mobile. fix it"", credited 60, `P` 40 and `LOOKUP` 20.

SM 2.5: "Each slot gets `focus_class` and `focus_project`: the app that held focus longest in the slot", and "A run of browser or chat-app slots counts as `LOOKUP`". SM 2.7 tests `focus_project(slot(τ))` for every candidate.

## Expected

`P` 40, from 20:02:00 to 20:02:30 and 20:02:50 to 20:03:00, and `LOOKUP` 20.

## What the rules give

| Slot | Browser | Terminal | Focus | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20:02:00, 20:02:15 | 0 s | 15 s | terminal | `P` 30, T2's lead |
| 20:02:30 | 15 s | 0 s | browser | `LOOKUP` 15 |
| 20:02:45 | 5 s | 10 s | terminal | `P` 15, T2's lead |

So `P` 45 and `LOOKUP` 15. The minute still credits 60 s and the hour 1,490 s. Only the split moves: `P` 495 and `LOOKUP` 195 for the hour.

No timing gives SM's split, because a lookup is made of whole slots, so its credit is a multiple of 15 s until the 180 s cap. The other lookups in 6.5 fit: 20:04:00-:30 and 20:38:00-:30 are two slots each, and 20:41:10-:40 holds the slots 20:41:15 and 20:41:30, which is SM's 30 s.

## What the fixture does

The browser has focus from 20:02:30.000 to 20:02:50.000, SM's times. Row 20:02 keeps SM's `P` 40 and `LOOKUP` 20 and names this erratum. gen.py checks that the slot 20:02:30 is the browser's and the slots on either side are the terminal's.
