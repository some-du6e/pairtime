## Computed

The quoted texts have 73 and 60 characters. Both are plain ASCII, so characters, bytes and UTF-16 units agree.

| Prompt | SM's length | Lead from it | Quoted text's length | Lead from it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23:40:00, the `/goal` | 75 | 120 + 50 = 170 s, from 23:37:10 | 73 | 120 + 48.67 = 168.67 s, from 23:37:11.33 |
| 07:37:00, the follow-up | 61 | 120 + 40.67 = 160.67 s, which SM rounds to 161, from 07:34:19 | 60 | 120 + 40 = 160 s, from 07:34:20 |

With the quoted texts, 23:37 becomes `P` 48.67 and `X.no_anchor` 11.33 (or 49 and 11 if the edge rounds to the nearest second), and the night's credit 808.67 s (or 809) instead of 810. 07:34 becomes `READ` 20 and `P` 40, which leaves its 60 s unchanged.

A second, smaller point. 61 / 1.5 = 40.67 s, and SM rounds it to 41, so the lead starts on a whole second. SM 1.3 says window edges are applied to the second, but not which way they round. 07:34:19 needs rounding to the nearest second or down; rounding up would give 07:34:20.

## What the fixture does

The `prompt.submit` events carry SM's lengths, `len_chars` and `typed_len_est` 75 and 61, so every row and the 810 s total are SM's, and the plan's acceptance numbers hold. `prompts.json` quotes both texts word for word and marks each entry with this erratum. A feature computed from text, such as a MinHash, uses the quote; the lead uses the event's length. Rows 23:37 and 07:34 in `expected.json` name this erratum.
