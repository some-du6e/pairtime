Date 2026-11-19. Raised by WP-FX1. Status: decided 2026-11-20 by the orchestrator for Karim: the quotes were wrong and SM's numbers stand. SM 6.4 now quotes texts of 75 and 61 characters. SM 1.3 now says that a lead's typing part and the reading part of RETURN_CREDIT round to the nearest second, halves up, and that a second belongs to a window, a turn's run, a tail or the reading horizon when the second's start falls inside it, as pkg/score already does. No row or total changes.

## Computed

The quoted texts have 73 and 60 characters. Both are plain ASCII, so characters, bytes and UTF-16 units agree.

| Prompt | SM's length | Lead from it | Quoted text's length | Lead from it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23:40:00, the `/goal` | 75 | 120 + 50 = 170 s, from 23:37:10 | 73 | 120 + 48.67 = 168.67 s, from 23:37:11.33 |
| 07:37:00, the follow-up | 61 | 120 + 40.67 = 160.67 s, which SM rounds to 161, from 07:34:19 | 60 | 120 + 40 = 160 s, from 07:34:20 |

With the quoted texts, 23:37 becomes `P` 48.67 and `X.no_anchor` 11.33 (or 49 and 11 if the edge rounds to the nearest second), and the night's credit 808.67 s (or 809) instead of 810. 07:34 becomes `READ` 20 and `P` 40, which leaves its 60 s unchanged.

A second, smaller point. 61 / 1.5 = 40.67 s, and SM rounds it to 41, so the lead starts on a whole second. SM 1.3 says window edges are applied to the second, but not which way they round. 07:34:19 needs rounding to the nearest second or down; rounding up would give 07:34:20.

A feature computed from text, such as a MinHash, uses the quote; the lead uses the event's length. Rows 23:37 and 07:34 in `expected.json` name this erratum.

## Decision

Decided 2026-11-20 by the orchestrator for Karim.

1. The quotes were wrong. SM 6.4's 23:38 row now quotes `/goal all tests pass, and lighthouse performance above 90 on the home page.` (75 characters) and its 07:35 row "nice. explain what you changed in the service worker and why?" 2. SM 1.3 now says how the scorer counts seconds. A lead's typing part and the reading part of `RETURN_CREDIT` round to the nearest second, halves up, so 61 / 1.5 = 40.67 s is 41 s and 6.5's 35 / 1.5 = 23.33 s is 23 s. A second belongs to a window, a turn's run, a tail or the reading horizon when the second's start is at or after the span's start and before its end. `pkg/score` already works this way. The 07:34 row's 07:34:19 comes from rounding the typing part, and no fixture row depends on which way a mid-second edge would go.

The sections above describe SM and the fixtures before the decision.
