# Erratum: DESIGN 9.3 drafts Deflation Justification, which the handbook leaves to a person

Date 2026-11-20. Raised by the review of #13 (WP-PG0) in issue #44, written in PR #55. Status: decided 2026-11-24 by the orchestrator for Karim (Decision, below). pairtime drafts the text on the review page, the reviewer confirms it as written or rewrites it, and the field stays `filled_by: reviewer`. The justification endpoint returns only confirmed text. While a deflation has none, the endpoint answers `409 deflation_unconfirmed` and the Airtable script writes nothing. Question 1's yes is an interim answer until Quality and Integrity answers DESIGN 9.5's question 8, since the handbook is theirs. The design works under either answer, so nothing waits on them. DESIGN 9.3, 9.5 and 13.2, PLAN WP-PG1, WP-PG2, WP-PG12, WP-RV2 and WP-RV4, and `testdata/upstream/airtable/` change in one docs PR.

## Input

The handbook's Override Hours Spent Justification page has a table of the justification fields that says, for each one, when to include it and whether it can be automated. Deflation Justification goes in when the reviewer deflated the tracked hours, and the table asks for a person to write it, as it does for Specific Technical Features. It says the Hackatime project names and dates, the Hackatime id and the Lapse links can or should be automated, that the explanation in Alternate Tracking Method and Additional Justification may be, and that Specific Technical Features and Deflation Justification should be human-written. `testdata/upstream/handbook/fields.json` records the page as retrieved on 2026-11-19, with `written_by: human` for this field. Fetched again on 2026-11-20, the page has the same SHA-256 (`1bff585e...`).

DESIGN 9.3's field table fills Deflation Justification with "a factual draft from the reason codes (...) that the reviewer confirms or rewrites", and the review page shows that draft (DESIGN 7.7 item 11). WP-PG1 puts the draft in the justification endpoint. WP-PG2's Airtable script writes the mapped fields from that endpoint, and its CI check has "every mapped field filled from the golden justification".

Until PR #55, `airtable/fields.json` marked the wrangler column `Justification - Deflation Justification` as `filled_by: pairtime`. Its schema defines that as text from the justification endpoint, written by the Airtable script or pasted by the reviewer.

## Expected

The field holds text a person wrote.

## What DESIGN gives

Text pairtime wrote. A reviewer who confirms the draft sends pairtime's sentence under their own name. Nothing in DESIGN 9.3 or WP-PG2 ties the script to that confirmation either, so a default mapping that includes the field can write the draft into Airtable before anyone has read it, which DESIGN 7.7 doesn't intend.

The draft also describes a different deflation. The handbook's examples give a reviewer's reasons for cutting hours, such as features that don't match the submitter's experience. pairtime's draft says what pairtime's scoring left out of Hackatime's hours, which the Alternate Tracking Method text already says ("For this window Hackatime logged 9.1 h ... pairtime credited 5.9 h").

## What the capture does now

`airtable/fields.json` marks the column `filled_by: reviewer`, like Specific Technical Features. Its note says the handbook asks for a person, that DESIGN 9.3 has pairtime draft the text for the reviewer, and that until this is settled the Airtable script leaves the field out of its default mapping while the reviewer sees the draft on the review page. `airtable/PROVENANCE.md` names it as the one field DESIGN 9.3 fills that isn't marked `pairtime`. WP-PG2 takes DESIGN 9.3's table as its oracle along with this capture, so this erratum is what keeps the field out of its default mapping for now.

## For Karim

   - No, which is how the capture now reads the handbook. DESIGN 9.3's row becomes "written by the reviewer; the review page shows pairtime's factual draft", and WP-PG2 leaves the field out of its default mapping.
   - Yes. Then the reviewer confirms or edits the draft on pairtime's review page before the endpoint returns it, so the script never writes an unconfirmed draft. DESIGN 9.3 and WP-PG1 gain that step, and `airtable/fields.json` goes back to `filled_by: pairtime` in a PR of its own.

## Decision

Decided 2026-11-24 by the orchestrator for Karim.

1. Yes, as an interim answer. The handbook is Quality and Integrity's, so only they can settle what it means by "should be human-written". Under AGENTS.md's rule for such questions, DESIGN 9.5 asks them (question 8), as point 2 proposed, and 13.2 says what pairtime does until they answer (item 17). Until then, a draft the reviewer confirms unchanged counts as written by a person, because the reviewer adopts it. They read it on the review page after their cuts, confirm it under their own opaque reviewer id, and could have rewritten it. pairtime reads the handbook as asking for a person who answers for the hours deflated to and the reasons, and typing the same numbers again would add work and no judgment. Ari has its reviewers write both prose fields before they approve or deflate, and offers them no draft (HC 3.6). pairtime instead has the reviewer adopt or rewrite its draft before the field can leave pairtime.
   - The field stays `filled_by: reviewer`, and `airtable/fields.schema.json` keeps its rule that refuses `pairtime` for it. No PR puts it back to `pairtime`, since the endpoint carries the reviewer's text, not pairtime's.
   - The endpoint never returns a draft. While the review deflates the tracked hours and no confirmation holds, `GET .../justification?format=handbook` answers `409 deflation_unconfirmed` with the review URL, and the Airtable script writes no field of that record. Once a confirmation holds, the endpoint returns its text byte for byte. With no deflation and none holding, the field comes back empty. So no mapping can write a draft nobody has read.
   - The script's default mapping includes `Justification - Deflation Justification` with the five fields marked `pairtime`, because only confirmed text can reach it.
   - If Quality and Integrity says an adopted draft isn't human-written, a new erratum moves the draft out of the reviewer's text box to sit beside an empty one. WP-RV4 keeps, with each confirmation, whether it took the draft unchanged, so pairtime can list the records to fix. The endpoint, its 409 and the script stay as they are.
2. The draft states the deflation the program's record shows. The tracked hours are the larger of pairtime's credited hours and, when the teen linked Hackatime, Hackatime's figure from the Alternate Tracking Method text. The approved hours are the `Optional - Override Hours Spent` figure. The draft gives both to 0.1 h, as DESIGN 9.3's example does, and a deflation counts only when the approved hours are below the tracked hours at that rounding. DESIGN 9.3's quoted draft treats the step from Hackatime's figure down to pairtime's as a deflation, so pairtime's exclusions belong in the text when the draft starts from Hackatime's figure. But that draft always ends at pairtime's figure, so it gives the wrong number for any review with a cut. And where pairtime credits more than Hackatime logged, as in SM 6.1, the step from Hackatime's figure to pairtime's is no deflation, while a reviewer's cut below pairtime's figure is one. So the draft runs from the tracked hours to the approved hours. When it starts from Hackatime's figure, it lists pairtime's exclusions by reason code, then any hours no reason code explains. Each cut, raise or clearance follows with its reason code and hours. The draft copies none of the reviewer's own text, so an `other` cut shows its hours alone, and the reviewer can add why in the box. Its rounded parts add up to the deflation.
3. pairtime keeps each confirmation with a hash of the facts its draft stated: the tracked hours and whose they are, the approved hours, and each part's hours. A confirmation holds only while pairtime's draft states the same facts. A cut, raise, clearance, re-score or purge that moves any of them ends it, and the review page drafts again. `POST .../review` refuses a confirmation sent against facts that have since moved, a text in which `pkg/redact`, run with the teen's handle among its known values, finds a span, and, for a deflation, a text that doesn't give the approved hours. DESIGN 4.4 keeps this text "written without personal data", and the handbook asks for "the number of hours deflated to".
4. `POST .../outcome` gets no new 409. The endpoint's 409 is what keeps an unconfirmed draft out of Airtable. An outcome gate would also block rejected outcomes, and a decision pairtime refused to record would leave the submission's spot-check window at its freeze (DESIGN 4.4).

WP-ERR1 applies the text to DESIGN 9.3, 9.5 and 13.2, to PLAN WP-PG1, WP-PG2, WP-PG12, WP-RV2 and WP-RV4 and the submission line of PLAN's dependency list, and to `testdata/upstream/airtable/` in one docs PR. WP-PG1 builds the draft, the endpoint's 409 and its golden answers. WP-RV4 keeps the confirmation, WP-RV2 puts the draft in a text box on the review page, and WP-PG2's script writes nothing on any answer but a 200. Whichever of WP-PG1 and WP-RV4 lands second makes the endpoint return a kept confirmation while its facts hold, and whichever of WP-PG1, WP-RV2 and WP-RV4 lands last builds the text box. WP-PG12's round trip waits for both.
