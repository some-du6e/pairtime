# Erratum: a slot of unknown focus can credit two projects, and a minute bucket's slot names one

Date 2026-11-24. Raised by WP-FX8, the focus-unknown fixtures. Status: decided 2026-11-24 by the orchestrator for Karim: option 2 (D-split-slots). In a slot of unknown focus, the project whose candidate credits the slot's first credited second is the slot's p. From then on, only that project's windows and buckets credit its seconds. A second none of them credits takes SM 5.2's codes, without X.other_focus or X.ambiguous. Relayed credit keeps one project to a slot the same way, as pkg/score already does. SM keeps its wording; this erratum records the reading.

## Input

- SM 2.5, the focus-unknown paragraph: a slot of unknown focus passes every focus test "for every project", and "Where candidates of several projects want a second, 3.3's rules 2 to 6 choose among them, and buckets of different projects go to the project whose human anchor qualifying the drain (2.4) is nearest the second, the earlier anchor on a tie, then the lower `project_key`." It decides second by second.
- SM 3.3 rule 1 lets every candidate survive in such a slot. Wherever focus is known, the rule keeps only candidates of the slot's `focus_project`, so a slot credits one project, apart from a lookup, which is a whole number of slots, and a returned slot, whose focus had to stay on the anchor's project.
- SM 5.1: each of a minute bucket's four slots has one project, its credited seconds, its dominant reason, its activity and its level. DESIGN 4.3's tier-1 bucket is `slots[4]{p, cr, why, kind, ev, lbl}`, and the server's per-project totals come from each slot's `p` and `cr`.
- [The ambiguous-anchors erratum](2026-11-20-sm-3.2-ambiguous-anchors.md) met the same clash for ambiguous slots, where every project's windows compete, and its decision keeps one project a slot: "The project whose window credits the slot's first credited second is the slot's `p` (SM 5.1). From then on only that project's windows credit the slot's seconds". Neither SM 2.5 nor D-focus-unknown says whether that carries over to a slot of unknown focus.

## Expected

Each slot's credit fits its minute bucket, so the server's per-project totals are the device's (SM 3.1: "Every second is credited to at most one project, so per-project totals always add up to the user's total").

## What the rules give

Second by second, a slot of unknown focus can split between projects. [`testdata/timelines/focus-unknown/d-two-projects`](../../testdata/timelines/focus-unknown/d-two-projects/) has two Claude Code sessions, `site` and `api`, on GNOME on Wayland, and two such slots:

- **16:03:30 to 16:03:45.** `site`'s bucket is empty until its edit lands at 16:03:40, so `api`'s drains 10 s. From 16:03:40 `site`'s prompt A1 is the nearer anchor, so `site` drains 5 s.
- **16:08:00 to 16:08:15.** At 16:08:00 A1 is 300 s back and `api`'s prompt B2 301 s ahead, so `site` drains 1 s. From 16:08:01 B2 is nearer, so `api` drains 14 s.

A bucket that names one project for either slot either credits the other project's seconds to it on the server or drops them.

Read per second, as SM 2.5 alone says, 16:03 credits `site` SUP 50 and `api` SUP 10, and 16:08 `site` READ 1 and `api` READ 59, so `site` gets 351 s and `api` 485. The fixture follows the decision below instead.

## For Karim

Answered on 2026-11-24 by the decision below.

1. Keep SM 2.5 per second, and let a minute bucket's slot carry credit for more than one project. That changes more than the tier-1 bucket: SM 5.1's `slots[4]`, one project, credit, reason and level a slot; `pkg/event`'s `BucketSlot` and `Tier1SlotFields`; DESIGN 4.3's tier-1 table, which ground rule 10 sends through Karim; ingest-v1's shape check of a bucket's slots; DESIGN 6.5's CC6, which compares the live and re-scored buckets slot by slot; SM 3.4's merge across devices, which keeps one device's slot over another's; and `pkg/score`'s `Credit.ProjectKey`, one project a slot. The server's rollups would follow.
2. Or carry D-ambiguous-anchors over: in a slot of unknown focus, the project whose candidate credits the slot's first credited second takes the slot, and from then on only that project's windows and buckets credit its seconds. In d-two-projects, `api` keeps the slot from 16:03:30, so its bucket drains 15 s there and `site`'s waits until 16:03:45. `site` then drains through 16:08:15, keeping the slot from 16:08:00, and `api` reads from 16:08:15 to B2's lead at 16:10:01, 106 s. Rows 16:03 and 16:08 become `site` SUP 45 with `api` SUP 15, and `site` READ 15 with `api` READ 45. `site` gets 360 s and `api` 476, the same 836 in all. The fixture's rows 16:03 and 16:08 name this erratum, and its README, `slots.json` and totals would change with them.

A relayed candidate meets the same question. SM 2.7 lets it take a second no other candidate wants, "whatever the slot's presence and focus", so one slot could hold this device's credit for one project and a relayed lead or relayed reading for another. `pkg/score` already keeps relayed credit to one project a slot. The relayed fixtures keep one session at a time and don't reach it.

## Decision

Decided 2026-11-24 by the orchestrator for Karim: option 2, D-split-slots, for the reasons it gives D-ambiguous-anchors. It keeps SM 5.1's one project a slot, so SM 5.1's `slots[4]`, DESIGN 4.3, ingest-v1's slot shape, CC6's slot compare and SM 3.4's per-slot merge stay as they are, and Karim needs no tier-1 change. `pkg/score` keeps one project a slot already (`Credit.ProjectKey`), relayed credit included.

1. In a slot of unknown focus, the project whose candidate credits the slot's first credited second is the slot's `p`, and from then on only that project's windows and buckets credit the slot's seconds. SM 2.5's rules, including the nearest qualifying anchor for buckets of different projects, still pick that first second's project.
2. A second of the slot that none of them credits takes SM 5.2's codes, without `X.other_focus` and `X.ambiguous`.
3. `focus-unknown/d-two-projects` follows it: `api` takes the slot from 16:03:30 and `site` the slot from 16:08:00, so 16:03 is `site` SUP 45 and `api` SUP 15, 16:08 is `site` READ 15 and `api` READ 45, and `site` gets 360 s and `api` 476, still 836 in all. Its `slots.json` gives each slot its `p`.

SM keeps its wording; this erratum records the reading.
