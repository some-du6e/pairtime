# Erratum: SM 6.1's cycles 9 and 10 never reach the same-error limit

Date 2026-11-19. Raised by WP-FX3. Status: waiting for Karim. No credit depends on it: SM and its rules agree that nothing in 6.1 is discounted, and `testdata/timelines/6.1` keeps SM's rows.

## Input

SM 6.1's third variation: "In cycles 9 and 10 the agent's test run fails three times in a row with the same `AssertionError`. Rowan answers each failure with a new hypothesis ... The fix-it rule only discounts error pastes and go-aheads (4.2), so nothing is discounted. His own edit fixes it, and the next run is a `run_green`." docs/PLAN.md gives WP-SC6 the check "6.1's cycles 9 and 10 stay undiscounted".

## Expected

The error recurs often enough to reach `SAME_ERROR_LIMIT` = 3 before one of Rowan's prompts, so the exemption for substantive prompts is what keeps his credit.

## What the rules give

SM 4.2 counts `occurrences(e)` "since the last human change event", and a typed edit (`E`) and a human commit (`G`) are both change events. Every cycle's minute 11 is one or the other, and it falls between that cycle's turn and the next cycle's prompt. So the count is 0 at every prompt in 6.1, whatever the turns hold. In the fixture it reaches 2 in cycle 9's turn, at 15:41:50 and 15:42:20, goes back to 0 at the commit at 15:47:40, reaches 1 at 15:54:20 and goes back to 0 at his edit at 15:59:40. No other placement of the three failures does better, since each cycle has one prompt and a change event before the next.

The sentence "Rowan answers each failure with a new hypothesis" doesn't fit the shape either. With one prompt per cycle, at most one prompt falls between failures, cycle 10's at 15:52:00. Cycle 9's prompt comes before the first failure, and his answer to the last one is his own edit.

So nothing is discounted, as SM says, but because the count resets and not because his prompts are substantive. Error pastes in their place would be credited too, and WP-SC6's 6.1 check passes without touching the exemption.

## What the fixture does

The agent's test runs fail after each of cycle 9's edits, at 15:41:50 and 15:42:20, and after cycle 10's second edit, at 15:54:20, all with one `error_sig`. Cycle 10's prompt carries SM's quoted hypothesis. His hand edit is at 15:59:40, and cycle 11's run passes at 16:06:20. No row names this erratum, since no credit is in question.
